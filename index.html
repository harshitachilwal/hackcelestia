Welcome to Hack Celestia – the fifth season of our hackathon!     8. Hardware Related

  8. Intelligent Satellite Power Management:

    Satellites operate with limited and highly variable energy generated through solar panels, making efficient power management essential for uninterrupted mission operations. How can innovative hardware systems intelligently monitor, distribute, store, and optimize onboard power while ensuring critical satellite components remain operational during energy shortages?

  9. Autonomous Satellite Attitude Control:

    Maintaining the correct orientation of a satellite is essential for communication, navigation, imaging, and efficient solar power generation. How can advanced sensing and actuator-based hardware systems enable satellites to autonomously detect orientation changes and stabilize themselves with minimal dependence on ground control?

  10. Next-Generation Satellite Thermal Management:

    Spacecraft are exposed to extreme temperature variations, with components potentially facing overheating or severe cooling depending on their orientation and mission conditions. How can innovative thermal hardware systems monitor and regulate spacecraft temperatures to protect sensitive components and maintain reliable operation in extreme space environments?

  11. Space Debris Detection & Collision Protectiont:

    The increasing population of satellites and orbital debris creates a growing threat of collisions that can damage or completely disable spacecraft. How can compact sensing and tracking hardware enable satellites to detect nearby objects, estimate collision risks, and support timely avoidance or protective responses?

  12. Autonomous Satellite Fault Detection:

    A failure in a single spacecraft subsystem can compromise an entire satellite mission, while communication delays can make immediate intervention from Earth difficult. How can onboard hardware systems continuously monitor critical components, identify abnormal behaviour, and autonomously isolate or respond to faults before they escalate?

  13. Satellite-Based Disaster Sensing Hardware:

    Rapid detection of floods, wildfires, landslides, and other disasters requires reliable environmental sensing over large geographical areas. How can compact satellite payloads and sensor systems be designed to capture critical environmental parameters while minimizing power consumption, hardware complexity, and data transmission requirements?

GUIDELINES
  • Each team must consist of 4-5 members.
  • Teams must include at least 2 female members.
  • Only students enrolled in colleges are eligible to participate.
  • Teams must be formed from members within the same college.
  • Teams can include participants from different branches and different years within the same college.
  • Only female participants are allowed to attend the hackathon in person at the Banasthali campus.
  • Male team members can participate and assist online.
  • Teams comprised entirely of female members are welcome and encouraged to participate.
  • All branches and courses are eligible for participation—diverse backgrounds are encouraged.
  • There is no limit on the number of teams from a single college.
  • Teams are permitted to submit multiple entries for different domains, provided each submission has a different team leader.
  • Each member can only be part of one team per domain
  • Each team can submit only one idea per domain.

FAQs
Is this hackathon an online event or offline?
For teams outside of Banasthali, only female participants are allowed to attend the event in person, while male team members can assist their team online during the offline event.
Is it compulsory to attend the event offline?
Yes, all female team members are required to attend the event in person for the offline round at the Banasthali Campus.
Does this hackathon have any participation fees for students?
No. The hackathon is free of cost for any student from any college all over the world.
Are there any special arrangements for international participants?
Yes,international participants can join in physical/hybrid mode.
Can I participate with other college teams?
No, teams should have members from the same college itself. Inter-college teams are not allowed.
What are the perks and benefits of participating in this hackathon?
Hackathons provide hands on experience with the experts from different field working on the projects like Blockchain, AI/ML, Machine Learning, etc. This will help to give a head start if any one wants to make project on the field. Along with that the participant will be provided with cool swags and stickers.
What are the Accommodation Facilities for the participants from outside the college?
Participants will be provided with accommodation and food in the Guest House of the College during the Hackathon Period.
How to reach Banasthali Vidyapith for the event?
The nearest airport to Banasthali Vidyapith is Jaipur Airport (JAI) which is 50.2 km away from the campus.From Jaipur you can easily find cabs or buses from Sindhi Camp(Bus stand) to reach our campus.The nearest Railway Station is Niwai Station which is 5 km away from the campus.
